# LinkedIn and X posts

## LinkedIn

> I built my first prototype: **PhoneKey Vault**, a backup where your phone is the key to your laptop.
>
> Why: too many students lose a laptop and their whole final-year project with it. And in Ghana, restoring a 200 GB laptop over mobile data could cost $80–$192.
>
> How it works:
> - Your PC backs up into an encrypted vault (built on restic).
> - The master key lives only on your phone.
> - A new PC can't restore until you tap Approve on your phone.
> - If your laptop is stolen, you lock it out of your vault for good.
>
> It's open source, with a working prototype, end-to-end tests and a white paper. Next is a native phone app and a pilot with 30 students on one campus.
>
> I'd love feedback, and I'm looking for anyone who'd like to help build the Flutter app.
> 🔗 https://github.com/demi956/phonekey-vault
>

## X (Twitter) thread

1. I built a backup where your phone is the key to your laptop. Lose the laptop, keep your work. 🧵 https://github.com/demi956/phonekey-vault
2. The problem: students lose laptops, and their only copy of their projects with them. In Ghana, restoring 200 GB over mobile data costs $80–$192.
3. The fix: your PC backs up into an encrypted vault. The master key lives only on your phone. A new PC can't restore until you tap Approve.
4. Stolen laptop? One approval on your phone removes its key, and it can never open your vault again.
5. It's built on restic, about 380 lines of Python, MIT-licensed, with end-to-end tests. Next: a Flutter app and a campus pilot. Feedback welcome!
